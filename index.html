01 / ABOUT ME

More than a designer. A creative builder.

I’m Olayeye Emmanuel, a Nigerian graphic designer and creative builder with several years of experience creating visual communication for brands, businesses, products and personal projects.

My work sits at the intersection of design, technology, AI and entrepreneurship. I'm interested in more than making things look good. I enjoy understanding a problem, finding a clear direction and turning an idea into something people can actually see, understand and use.

I started with graphic design and gradually developed my skills across tools such as Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, CorelDRAW and Canva.

Over time, my interest expanded beyond individual designs into branding, digital experiences, product communication, creative strategy and technology.

Today, I'm continuing to explore how design and technology can work together. I experiment with AI tools, digital products, websites, branding systems and new ways of building and communicating ideas.
